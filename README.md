# Task 02: Revenue Forecasting & Price Sensitivity

## Overview
This project analyzes historical revenue and demand dynamics, estimates price elasticity of demand, projects a 6-month revenue baseline (`2026-01` to `2026-06`), and simulates pricing scenarios (**+5% price increase** and **-10% price discount**) for the CadetX Data Science internship.

## Key Findings
- **Price Elasticity of Demand:** `-0.533` (**price-inelastic**, meaning demand changes by ~`0.533%` for every `1%` change in price).
- **6-Month Baseline Revenue:** `122,794.56`
- **+5% Price Scenario Revenue:** `125,670.06` (**+2.34%** lift over baseline)
- **-10% Price Scenario Revenue:** `116,898.18` (**-4.80%** impact vs. baseline)

## Files Included
- `revenue_time_series.csv` — Daily price, demand, and revenue dataset.
- `monthly_revenue_summary.csv` — Monthly aggregated revenue and trend forecast.
- `revenue_forecast_scenarios.csv` — 6-month baseline and `+5%` / `-10%` scenario projections.
- Power BI report (`.pbix`) — Executive dashboard with KPI cards, elasticity, monthly trends, and scenario comparisons.
